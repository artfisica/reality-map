# Claim and Source Ledger

## The Civilian Geopolitical Reality Map

**Author: Arturo Sánchez Pineda**  
**Edition: 14 October 2026**

This ledger is the evidentiary control for the manual and case studies. “Permitted wording” is language supported by the cited record. “Avoid” identifies claims that are broader than the evidence or that confuse distinct legal categories. The ledger should be updated whenever a court rules, a policy changes or new primary evidence becomes available.

## Editorial and authorial standard

The project combines two forms of accountability.

**Scientific accountability** requires explicit categories, reproducible comparisons, stated scope conditions, rival explanations and conclusions that can be revised by new evidence. A number must retain its source’s definition; an estimate must not become an observed count; temporal sequence must not be presented as proof of causation.

**Authorial accountability** First person is appropriate when identifying standpoint, moral responsibility, fear, pain or the reason a question is being asked. It must not replace documentary evidence. [*Miedo*](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/miedo-arturo-s%C3%A1nchez-pineda-bhf8e/) and [*Dolor*](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/dolor-arturo-s%C3%A1nchez-pineda-zu1se/) are therefore authorial context, not proof of geopolitical facts.

The project’s voice retains five features of those articles:

1. **personal implication:** the author does not place himself outside the fear, distance or cowardice being examined;
2. **rejection of “I told you so”:** an earlier warning establishes chronology, not moral superiority after tragedy;
3. **concrete human consequence:** behind “civilians,” “damage” and “displacement” are families, homes, bodies and children;
4. **direct moral language:** killing, fear, pain, cowardice and double standard may be used when the category and evidence are explained;
5. **evidentiary discipline:** emphatic repetition and first person belong to the voice; grammatical errors, confusion between courts or accusations broader than the record do not.

Objectivity means that allies, adversaries and Venezuela’s own rulers receive the same evidentiary test. It does not require neutrality between a documented use of unlawful force and the civilians subjected to it.

## Editorial controls

Before publication, every draft should pass eight checks:

1. **Court check:** Does it confuse an investigation, warrant, provisional order or final judgment?
2. **Status check:** Does it call coercion, administration or intervention an “occupation” without effective-control evidence?
3. **Attribution check:** Does it turn a government’s assertion into an established fact?
4. **Causation check:** Does it assign a multi-actor catastrophe to one actor without evidence?
5. **Consequence check:** Does it say “no action” when the more accurate finding is action below the level of deterrence?
6. **Sequence check:** Is chronology being used as evidence of causation without an identified mechanism?
7. **Counterevidence check:** Does the text state material facts that weaken or limit its preferred conclusion?
8. **Voice check:** Is first person identifying standpoint and responsibility, or improperly substituting emotion for evidence?

The project’s language should be morally direct because its evidence is disciplined. The strength of the accusation must never exceed the strength of the record. Nor should euphemism make the language weaker than the record permits.
